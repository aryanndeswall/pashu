## 1. What was built

1. **SIH Hackathon 1-Click Header Demo Role Switcher (`HeaderBar.tsx`):**
   - Added interactive Role Pill Badge in the persistent header:
     - `👨‍🌾 पशुपालक` (Emerald)
     - `🩺 पशुवैद्य` (Blue)
     - `🏛️ अधिकारी` (Purple)
   - Tapping opens a `FluidDrawer` titled **"भूमिका बदला (SIH Demo Role Switcher)"** with 1-tap switching buttons for all 3 seeded personas without typing passwords.

2. **Role-Filtered Dynamic Navigation (`BottomBar.tsx`):**
   - **Consumer (Farmer):** 3 tabs (`Report` [लक्षणे नोंदवा], `Dashboard` [स्थानिक स्थिती], `Animals` [माझे पशु]) + SOS. Restricted `Labs` tab is completely hidden.
   - **Doctor (Field Vet):** 4 tabs (`Report` [८ लक्षणे], `Dashboard` [डॅशबोर्ड], `Animals` [पशु आधार], `Labs` [प्रयोगशाळा]) + SOS.
   - **Admin (DVO):** 4 tabs (`Dashboard` [कमांड सेंटर], `Report` [क्लिनिकल सारांश], `Animals` [नोंदणी], `Labs` [लॅब पडताळणी]) + SOS.

3. **Safety Navigation Guard (`navigationStore.ts` & `App.tsx`):**
   - Added `validateTabForRole(role)` in `navigationStore.ts`: switching to `consumer` while on the `labs` tab automatically redirects `activeTab` to `'report'`.
   - Updated `App.tsx` with a bilingual persona welcome banner (`स्वागत आहे, रमेश पाटील (राहुरी खुर्द)`) and a quick toggle to open the full `RoleSelectionView`.
   - On boot, `initSession()` restores the persisted role and profile from native SQLite.
